Frequently Asked Questions

Verified guidelines covering capital logistics, diplomatic visa access, hospital emergency response, and traveler services.

Islamabad operates a modern, high-frequency Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) network with flat fares starting from PKR 50. The Red Line runs from Rawalpindi Saddar to Pak Secretariat through Blue Area and PIMS Hospital. The Orange Line links Peshawar Morr directly to Islamabad International Airport in 35 minutes. CDA Electric Feeder Buses circulate throughout Sectors F-6, F-7, F-8, F-10, G-9, G-11, and I-8.
Key Highlights:
  • Red Line: Rawalpindi Saddar ⇄ Pak Secretariat (stops at PIMS Hospital & Blue Area).
  • Orange Line: Rapid direct connection to Islamabad International Airport (ISB).
  • CDA Electric Fleet: Eco-friendly feeder routes linking inner sector markets and universities.
  • Fare: Flat PKR 50 with electronic smart cards or token counters at each station.
Islamabad International Airport (ISB) is located 25 km west of central sectors (F-6, F-7, Blue Area). You can travel via the Orange Line Metro (Terminal Level 1 departure, PKR 50), on-demand inDrive and Yango app rides (PKR 1,800 – 2,800), or pre-book a dedicated chauffeur sedan through our 24/7 concierge for executive meet-and-greet with luggage assistance.
Transfer Summary:
  • Metro Bus (Orange): Economical PKR 50 terminal transit to Srinagar Highway interchange.
  • inDrive & Yango: Readily available at International & Domestic arrival lanes.
  • Executive Chauffeur Sedan: Door-to-door greeting with luggage assistance (0345-7059286).
Foreign embassies (US, UK, Schengen, UAE, Saudi Arabia, China, etc.) are situated within the secured Diplomatic Enclave in Sector G-5. General private vehicles are strictly restricted. Visitors must enter exclusively via the official Diplomatic Shuttle Service at Avenue 3 Gate.
Security & Entry Guidelines:
  • Terminal Location: Diplomatic Shuttle Terminal, Avenue 3, Sector G-5.
  • Mandatory Documents: Original CNIC or Passport + printed embassy appointment confirmation slip.
  • Shuttle Ticket: PKR 500 – 1,000 for standard or express air-conditioned shuttle transport.
  • Locker Storage: Cell phones, laptops, and large luggage are prohibited inside embassy waiting areas. Secure locker facilities are available at the shuttle station.
Key government attestation authorities are located in central sectors:
Authorized Centers:
  • HEC (Higher Education Commission): Sector H-9 (Undergraduate, Master & PhD degree attestation).
  • MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs): Constitution Avenue near Pak Secretariat (Marriage, birth & police clearance legalization).
  • IBCC (Inter Board Coordination): Sector G-10/4 (Matriculation and intermediate certificates).
In medical emergencies, dial 1122 immediately from any mobile phone or landline for free, rapid government paramedic and ambulance dispatch across Islamabad and Rawalpindi. For specialized care, direct your driver to PIMS Hospital (Sector G-8) for public tertiary emergency trauma, or Shifa International Hospital (Sector H-8) for private critical resuscitation and cardiology.
Key Emergency Corridors:
  • Emergency Ambulance: Dial 1122 for 24/7 free paramedic response.
  • PIMS Hospital (Sector G-8/3): National Burn Center, Children's Hospital & Trauma Center (Direct Metro stop).
  • Shifa International (Sector H-8/4): JCI-accredited tertiary care, emergency cardiac catheterization & stroke bay.
  • Maroof International (Sector F-10 Markaz): Executive emergency department with fast-track physician triage.
