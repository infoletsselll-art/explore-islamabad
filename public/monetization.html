Monetization & Custom Domain Deployment Guide

Practical instructions to launch on your domains visitislamabad.org and visitislamabad.xyz, activate Google AdSense, deploy on Firebase & Cloudflare, and publish to the Google Play Store.

💵 1. Monetize with Google AdSense (Web)

Earn passive revenue every time visitors view or click travel, flight, and hotel ads on your website.

  1. Sign up for Google AdSense: Visit adsense.google.com with your email info.visitislamabad@gmail.com.
  2. Add Your Domains: Enter visitislamabad.org and visitislamabad.xyz into your AdSense Sites dashboard.
  3. Insert Publisher ID: Replace ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX inside the <ins class="adsbygoogle"> tag in index.html with your approved Publisher ID.
  4. Upload ads.txt: Place a file named ads.txt in your root directory containing:
    google.com, pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0
  5. Direct Sponsorships: Charge local Islamabad guest houses, 4x4 fleet owners, and clinics a monthly featured listing fee (typically PKR 15,000 to PKR 50,000/month).
🔥 2. Deploy on Firebase & Link Custom Domains

The simplest way to host your high-speed static files with free SSL certificates on Google Cloud infrastructure.

  1. Install Firebase CLI & Deploy: Run in terminal:
    npm install -g firebase-tools
    firebase login
    firebase deploy --only hosting
  2. Connect Your Domains: In Firebase Console (console.firebase.google.com):
    • Go to Hosting ➔ Click Add Custom Domain.
    • Enter visitislamabad.org and visitislamabad.xyz.
  3. Update DNS Records: Log into where you bought your domains (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare, etc.) and add the A records provided by Firebase:
    Type: A | Name: @ | Value: 199.36.158.100
    Type: TXT | Name: @ | Value: (Firebase Verification Code)
    SSL certificates are issued automatically within 1 hour!
⚡ 3. Deploy on Cloudflare Pages

Ultra-fast global edge delivery with unlimited bandwidth and built-in DDoS protection.

  1. Log into dash.cloudflare.com.
  2. Click Workers & Pages ➔ Create Application ➔ Pages ➔ Upload Assets.
  3. Upload the generated cloudflare_pages_deploy.zip or drag-and-drop the public/ folder.
  4. Click Custom Domains ➔ Enter visitislamabad.org. Cloudflare configures the DNS instantly.
🖥️ 4. Deploy on Your Own Server / cPanel

If you have a Linux VPS or shared cPanel hosting server:

  1. Log into your cPanel ➔ Open File Manager.
  2. Navigate to public_html/.
  3. Upload all files from this project: index.html, services.html, rides.html, hotels.html, northern.html, ai-chat.html, maps.html, search.html, social.html, emergency.html, contact.html, and visit-islamabad.apk.
  4. Point your domain's Name Servers (NS) or A-record to your server IP address.
📱 5. Upload Android APK to Google Play Store

Make Visit Islamabad available to millions of Android smartphone users on the Google Play Store.

  1. Register Developer Account: Go to play.google.com/console and pay the one-time $25 registration fee.
  2. Create App: Click Create App ➔ App Name: Visit Islamabad ➔ Category: Travel & Local.
  3. Store Listing Details:
    • Short Description: 24/7 Federal Capital Visitor Gateway: Cabs, Guest Houses, Hospitals & AI Guide.
    • Full Description: Copy details from our about-visit-islamabad section.
    • Upload App Icon (512x512) and Feature Graphic (1024x500).
  4. Production Release: Go to Release ➔ Production ➔ Create New Release ➔ Upload the signed Android App Bundle (AAB) or APK from the project root.
  5. Review & Publish: Complete the Data Safety questionnaire (no location sold, user data encrypted) and submit for Google review. Your app goes live in 2–4 days!
