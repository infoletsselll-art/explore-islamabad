Emergency Services & Immediate Assistance

If you or someone in your group requires immediate medical rescue, police intervention, or road assistance in Islamabad, tap the quick dial buttons below.

🚑
Rescue 1122 (Ambulance & Fire)

Trained paramedics, advanced life support ambulances, and emergency medical transport across all Islamabad sectors.

📞 Dial 1122 (Toll Free)
👮
Islamabad Capital Police (15)

Immediate law enforcement, safety protection, lost child assistance, and tourist security patrol.

📞 Dial Police 15
🌟
Official Concierge & SOS Desk

24/7 dedicated assistance for out-of-city visitors, hospital admissions, late-night transit, and emergency lodging.

📞 Dial 0343 0673404
🛣️
National Motorway Police (130)

Road breakdown, towing, flat tire, and accident assistance on M-1, M-2, and Islamabad Expressway.

📞 Dial 130
🏥
Shifa Hospital Emergency ER

Direct emergency department triage line for cardiac, trauma, ICU, and pediatric emergency admissions.

📞 Connect Shifa ER Liaison
🚒
Capital Fire Brigade (16)

Rapid response firefighting and civil defense operations for buildings, markets, and Margalla forest fires.

📞 Dial 16 Fire Brigade
