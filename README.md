## 🌟 Overview
**Visit Islamabad** is the premier all-purpose platform designed for visitors from every province of Pakistan (Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, AJK) and international travelers arriving in Islamabad. 

Whether arriving for specialized hospital treatments, appearing before high judicial forums, taking university entrance examinations, shopping in federal markazes, or exploring northern transit, Visit Islamabad provides full-circle logistical assistance, verified accommodations, and transparent multi-budget transport.

## 🎯 Purpose-Specific Visitor Portals

### 🏥 1. Health & Medical Visitors
- **Hospitals Covered:** Shifa International Hospital (H-8), Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS G-8), Maroof International (F-10), Kulsum International (Blue Area), Quaid-e-Azam International (Peshawar Rd).
- **Logistics:** Ground-floor wheelchair-accessible guest houses, patient escort cabs, 24/7 emergency pharmacy guides, and diagnostic lab coordination.

### ⚖️ 2. Courts & Legal Appearances
- **Judicial Venues:** Supreme Court of Pakistan (Constitution Ave), Islamabad High Court (G-10), Federal Service Tribunal.
- **Logistics:** Red-zone protocol chauffeurs, parking clearance advisory, business hotels near the Federal Secretariat, and formal court dress code reminders.

### 🎓 3. University Exams & Student Transit
- **Campuses:** NUST (H-12), FAST-NUCES (H-9), Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU), COMSATS (Park Rd), Air University (E-9), Bahria University.
- **Logistics:** Flat **PKR 50** Metro Bus transit from bus terminals (Daewoo, Faizabad) right to campus gates, verified girls' and boys' hostels, and exam-center shuttle routes.

### 🛍️ 4. Shopping & Family Leisure
- **Venues:** The Centaurus Mall (F-8), Giga Mall (DHA II), Safa Gold Mall (F-7), Jinnah Super Market (F-7), Super Market (F-6), Aabpara Market (G-6), Melody Food Street.
- **Logistics:** Hourly shopping cabs, family suites, and curated dining guides.

### 🏔️ 5. Sightseeing & Gateway to Northern Areas
- **Capital Attractions:** Faisal Mosque, Daman-e-Koh, Pir Sohawa, Pakistan Monument, Lok Virsa Heritage Museum, Rawal Lake, Margalla Hills Hiking Trails (1 to 5).
- **Northern Transit:** Chauffeur-driven 4x4 Prado, Toyota Fortuner, and Hiace Grand Cabin vans connecting Islamabad to Murree, Nathia Gali, Kaghan, Naran, Swat Valley, Gilgit, Hunza, and Skardu.

## 🚀 Firebase Deployment Instructions

To deploy updates to Firebase Hosting:

```bash

npm install -g firebase-tools

# 2. Login to your Google / Firebase Account
firebase login

# 3. Deploy hosting assets (public/ directory)
firebase deploy --only hosting
```
